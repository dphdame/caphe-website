1. What California Data Show

State and county records on Medi-Cal losses, living costs, clinic fees, and county budgets.

Medi-Cal & Claims Minimum Group Sizes and Net Member Losses Help Counties Set Medi-Cal Call Lists

Uses a 100-member minimum and each group's net member loss to decide whom to call first.

 Food & Cost of Living Subtracting Local Rent From Pay Shows High Food Costs Above Poverty Limits

Checks CalFresh pay limits before rent next to area food shares after rent in four places.

 Food & Cost of Living High Rent and Local Poverty Explain Food Need and Diabetes Rates Better Than Store Miles

Compares estimated post-rent food shares with tract poverty models and store miles.

 Food & Cost of Living Tract Poverty Explains Most of Santa Clara's Food-Need Score Compared With Store Miles

Checks how poverty and store distance relate to food-need scores in Santa Clara and three counties.

 Food & Cost of Living Fixed Medi-Cal Visit Fees Do Not Meet City Clinic Costs While Poor Inland Areas Lack Care

Compares high clinic wages and rents in cities with high poverty rates in inland counties.

 Food & Cost of Living Changes in Data Counting Rules Change Food-Access Scores Even When Store Counts Stay the Same

Compares map searches, SNAP store lists, and county health permits across 408 census tracts.

 County Health Budgets Combining Pandemic Aid With Routine County Health Budgets Mixes Relief Grants With Pre-2020 Links

Tests grants sent during large death rises apart from 1-year and 13-year links before 2020.

 County Health Budgets Routine County Health Spending Links With Lower Death Rates Outside Pandemic Grant Years

Tests 2020-2023 emergency grants apart from 1-year and 13-year links before 2020.

2. Program Cost & Impact Models

"What-if" cost and staffing models that pair state pay and fee rules with trial results.

Community Health Workers Fixed Medi-Cal Visit Fees Pay Less Than One Tenth of County Care Worker Costs

Models visit fees and Year-1 bed savings in 58 counties if one worker serves 55 to 150 people.

 Medi-Cal & Claims In the Model, Most Medi-Cal Renewal Losses Come From Unreturned Mail Forms Over Work Rules

Models 2027 Medi-Cal losses for 120,239 adults if half of mailed forms go unreturned.

 Community Health Workers Five-Year Contracts Divide First-Year Care Team Setup Costs Across Five Years of Care

Models Year-1 setup costs versus five-year costs per healthy life year for a 10-worker team.

 Community Health Workers Distinct Healthy-Year and Death-Risk Units Help Counties Compare Care and Prevention

Reports healthy years gained and lower death risk, if causal, as two separate measures.

3. How to Check Policy Studies

Simple checks for reviewing study claims, risk scores, and multi-year budget plans before funding.

County Health Budgets Programs With Large Long-Term Health Gains Still Need Plans for Yearly Cash and Reserves

Compares long-term health value, if causal, with Year-1 cash costs and EDD's 25% reserve goal.

 Medi-Cal & Claims Planning Outreach, Costing State Laws, and Checking Budgets Each Need a Matched Study Method

Shows when to use matched survey records, time trends, county data, or call trials for budget choices.

 Medi-Cal & Claims Matching Paid Medicaid Claims With Federal and State Ban Lists Finds Providers One List Leaves Out

Matches paid Medicaid claims with federal and state ban lists and checks 50-month waits after claims.

 Medi-Cal & Claims Testing Fraud Checks Only on Current Billers Leaves Out Banned Providers Who Stopped Early

Compares fraud scores for all past billers with 2024 billers and adjusts alert rates to the full list.

 Community Health Workers Controlling for Post-Start Clinic Visits Subtracts the Care Step That Reduces Hospital Stays

Splits pre-start health risks from post-start clinic visits that help cut hospital stays.

 County Health Budgets County Health Spending Links Show Higher Value Than Cost Under Three Federal Rates If Causal

Compares costs per death avoided, if causal, with three federal rates and clinic pay offsets.

 County Health Budgets Wide Error Ranges Before a Policy Can Miss Slow Differences in County Trends

Checks error widths in prior trends and trend limits after a law across county data.

 Medi-Cal & Claims Two-Year Telehealth Counts Include Earlier Increases in Clinic Use Over Time

Compares 84 months of Medicaid billing counts with projected pre-2020 growth and other care.

 Medi-Cal & Claims Checking Telehealth With Other Medicaid Care Shows Distinct Growth Rates as More Adults Join

Checks Medicaid biller counts for telehealth and other care with projected pre-2020 trends.

 County Health Budgets Five-Year County Averages Separate Lasting Rural Death Risk From High One-Year Rates

Compares small-county rate changes and count-model variation with five-year county averages.
