Per-Visit Medi-Cal Rates Cover Only a Small Fraction of County Living-Wage CHW Salaries

How flat Medi-Cal visit pay compares with county worker pay, modeled care savings, and added healthy years.

When sizing a county Community Health Worker team, relying only on Medi-Cal visit billing leaves an 89% to 94% pay gap for field outreach.[1][2] Across all 58 state counties, one full-time CHW costs $92,684 to $122,102 per year.[2] Yet Medi-Cal pays a flat $31.74 per 30-minute visit.[1] In our four-model comparison, visit fees cover just 6% to 11% of field pay.[1][2] Adding modeled hospital savings and two-year health gains brings $407,006 to $852,900 in yearly value per worker.[1][3][4] Splitting hospital, clinic, and local outreach tracks helps county teams blend health plan funds, bed savings, and grants.[3][4]

• Flat Medi-Cal Pay vs. Local Worker Costs: Under DHCS SPA 22-0001, Medi-Cal pays a flat $31.74 per 30-minute health education visit across all 58 counties. Individual units pay $26.66. Local worker costs come from 2024 U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics QCEW county wages. Pay averages $54,000 base or $103,719 loaded statewide. Across 58 counties, total cost runs from $92,684 to $122,102. That sum includes 35% fringe, $19,000 lead support, $4,000 training, and 10% office cost.

• Medi-Cal Rates vs. Full Hospital Costs: Medi-Cal plan rates are $600 per ER visit, $7,000 per hospital stay, and $6,000 per readmission. Those numbers match DHCS fee tables. Full hospital costs are $2,100 per ER visit, $18,500 per stay, and $15,000 per readmission. Those numbers come from California HCAI and AHRQ HCUP cost files.

• Why Year-1 Hospital Bills Leave Out Scenario 4 Value (150 Adults in 10 High-Need ZIP Codes): Official 2024 HCAI Prevention Quality Indicators (PQI) log 279,905 avoidable stays in the state. The rate is 902.5 per 100,000 adults in PQI 90 and 715.3 in PQI 92. In high-need ZIP codes, 60% of adults speak a language other than English at home. Also, 14% of immigrant adults skip public aid out of fear. They face 2.4x higher odds of late care (Wolstein et al., 2022, JAMA Network Open). Reaching 150 adults stops 6.6 ER visits and 1.10 stays in Year 1 ($34,900 saved). It also adds 38 checks and brings 8.18 quality-adjusted life years (QALYs). That equals 0.0545 QALYs per adult over 2 years at a 3% discount rate. Each QALY is valued at $100,000 (Neumann et al., 2014, NEJM). After subtracting $34,900 in bed savings, Scenario 4 costs $7,771 per QALY gained ($63,569 / 8.18 QALYs). It brings $852,900/yr in total value (8.66:1).

Matching Each CHW Model to the Right Group, Goal & Budget

Local health leaders face two plain questions. First, how do counties pay a living wage ($92,684 to $122,102/yr) when Medi-Cal pays a flat $31.74 per 30-minute visit? Second, how should each model be judged? Judging every CHW team only by Year-1 bed bills favors small hospital teams. It shortchanges local outreach.

Weighing both Year-1 bed savings and two-year healthy years (QALYs) shows where each model fits best. Hospital teams serving 55 sick patients fit best when health plans and hospitals need fast ER and bed savings ($107,006 to $291,171/yr). Clinic teams serving 75 patients fit best when clinics bill for set visits ($46,561/yr, or 45.9% of pay) while cutting hospital bills ($182,333/yr). Local Promotora outreach across 150 adults in the 10 highest-need ZIP codes fits best for health agencies reaching families who delay care. It adds 8.18 healthy years ($818,000 value) plus $34,900 in bed savings. Total value reaches $852,900 (8.66:1) at $7,771 per QALY.

Mode Classification (Mode A Policy & Fiscal Model on Observed 58-County Baselines): This flagship study pairs state Medi-Cal fee rules, 2024 HCAI county stay counts, 2026 DHCS plan counts, and 2024 BLS QCEW county pay with trial effect sizes and two-year health weights. The unit of study is 1.0 FTE Community Health Worker across four annual caseloads. Those are 55 hospital patients, 75 clinic patients, or 150 adults across the 10 highest-need ZIP codes in a county.

Statutory Fee Schedule Provenance (Zero Regional Adjustment): California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS) State Plan Amendment 22-0001 sets flat state CHW rates for CPT 98960-98962. It pays $26.66 per 30-minute individual unit and $31.74 per 30-minute group education unit (modifier U2). There is no county wage adjustment. Visit counts, avoided stays, QALY gains, and net county balances are modeled.

58-County HCAI Avoidable Hospital Stay & BLS QCEW Wage Provenance: County stay rates come from the 2024 HCAI Prevention Quality Indicators (PQI) dataset. It covers N = 31,012,717 state adults with 279,905 avoidable stays (902.5 per 100,000 in PQI 90) and 221,844 chronic stays (715.3 per 100,000 in PQI 92). County wage tiers come from 2024 U.S. BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW) county pay. They are scaled to Santa Clara County Public Health FY 2026-27 wage rules ($59,000 base or $112,915 loaded in clinic; $54,000 base or $103,719 loaded statewide).

Trial Effect Sizes, Care Avoidance & QALY Utility Parameters: Hospital cuts (-22% ER visits, -30% hospital stays, -23% 30-day readmissions) come from the IMPaCT trial (Kangovi et al., 2020, Health Affairs). Two-year QALY gains equal 0.0545 QALYs per adult at a 3% discount rate (chronic disease 0.025, mental health 0.040, social needs 0.020). They are valued at $100,000 per QALY (Neumann, Cohen & Weinstein, 2014, N Engl J Med; Stansert Katzen et al., 2025, Lancet Primary Care). Care delay rates in immigrant areas (13.7% skip rate; OR = 2.39 for late care) come from Wolstein et al. (2022, JAMA Network Open). Staff turnover rates come from UCSF Healthforce Center surveys (N = 210).

Common Questions & Method Notes

Under which circumstances is each of the four CHW models best?
Hospital teams (55 high-need patients) work best when a health plan or county hospital needs fast Year-1 ER and bed savings. They save $107,006 at Medi-Cal rates or $291,171 at full bed cost. Clinic teams (75 patients) work best for clinics that can bill set 30-minute visits ($46,561/yr, or 45.9% of pay) while saving $182,333 in hospital costs. Local Promotora outreach (150 adults across 10 high-need ZIP codes) works best for public health teams reaching families who delay clinic care. It brings the largest health gain (+8.18 QALYs, worth $818,000) at a net cost of $7,771 per QALY.
Why is same-year hospital and emergency cost offset too narrow to evaluate neighborhood promotora prevention?
Local Promotoras reach families early, before blood sugar or high blood pressure leads to an ER trip. Counting only Year-1 hospital bills captures $34,900 in bed savings. Yet it leaves out 38 early checks per year and 8.18 added healthy years (QALYs) across 150 adults. Valuing health gains at $100,000 per QALY shows $852,900 in yearly value (8.66:1). The net cost is $7,771 per QALY gained, which is 92% below the $100,000 benchmark.
Does Medi-Cal adjust its CHW pay rate for high-cost California counties?
No. Under DHCS SPA 22-0001, Medi-Cal pays a flat state rate of $31.74 per 30-minute health education unit across all 58 counties. Individual units pay $26.66. Full-time CHW pay runs from $92,684 in rural counties to $103,719 statewide and $122,102 in Bay Area counties. As a result, field visit billing covers only 6% to 11% of yearly worker pay.
