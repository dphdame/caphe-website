Reserve Ratio Clarification: The Employment Development Department (EDD) defines 25% to 50% of annual disbursements as an operational adequate reserve rate target range under UIC §984 financing formula guidelines, not a mandatory statutory minimum. In fact, under EDD's baseline May 2026 forecast without expansion, the SDI fund operates below 25% in 2025 (18.1%) and 2026 (18.2%). Under the Expected scenario, the fund preserves positive cash balances throughout the projection period, reaching $6.37B (30.4% reserve ratio) by 2030.

Methodological Note on County Estimates: Public EDD monthly data reports aggregate statewide claims. County-level figures represent deterministic demographic apportionments based on official California Department of Finance population distributions scaled to verified statewide totals. These estimates illustrate local community impact and should be interpreted as allocated projections, not administrative case counts.
